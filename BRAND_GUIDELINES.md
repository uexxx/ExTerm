## Reserved marks

The following are reserved by ExTerm Labs and may not be used to identify a fork, hosted service or derived product:

- the name **ExTerm**
- the name **ExTerm.labs** and the handle **@exterm.labs**
- the **Candle X** logo mark: the gold ring of eight candlesticks arranged in an X around a central diamond, and the
  **EX**/**TERM** two-tone wordmark

## What you may do

- Refer to ExTerm by name in articles, reviews, tutorials and comparisons, linking to <https://github.com/uexxx/ExTerm>.
- Run **unmodified** official builds for your own personal, non-commercial use.
- Link others to the official releases page (instead of re-hosting the files).

## What you may not do

- Publish a modified build, hosted web wrapper, app-store listing, social account or domain under the ExTerm
  name or with the Candle X mark.
- Use a name or logo confusingly similar to the above (for example "ExTerm Pro", "ExTerminal", a recoloured Candle X).
- Imply endorsement by, or affiliation with, ExTerm Labs.
- Remove or alter the copyright and license notices in the source, the `LICENSE` file, or the in-app About notice.

## Forks and derivative works

Forks, clones, mirrors and derivative works of ExTerm v17.0+ are not permitted without express written permission
from ExTerm Labs. Any permitted derivative must use its own name and logo, with no Candle X mark or `@exterm.labs`
watermark.

## Official builds

Official releases are published only at <https://github.com/uexxx/ExTerm/releases> and ship a `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
See [How to verify official builds](README.md#-how-to-verify-official-builds).
