# ExTerm Brand & Logo Guidelines

The ExTerm **source code** is licensed under the [GNU AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The ExTerm **brand** is not.
The AGPL gives you the right to use, study, modify and redistribute the code. It does not give you the right
to present your version as the official ExTerm.

## Reserved marks

The following are reserved by ExTerm Labs and may not be used to identify a fork, hosted service or derived product:

- the name **ExTerm**
- the name **ExTerm.labs** and the handle **@exterm.labs**
- the **Candle X** logo mark: the gold ring of eight candlesticks arranged in an X around a central diamond, and the
  **EX**/**TERM** two-tone wordmark

## What you may do

- Say your project is "based on ExTerm" or "a fork of ExTerm", in plain text, with a link to
  <https://github.com/uexxx/ExTerm>.
- Refer to ExTerm by name in articles, reviews, tutorials and comparisons.
- Run and redistribute **unmodified** official builds under their original name, with the AGPL notices intact.

## What you may not do

- Publish a modified build, hosted web wrapper, app-store listing, social account or domain under the ExTerm
  name or with the Candle X mark.
- Use a name or logo confusingly similar to the above (for example "ExTerm Pro", "ExTerminal", a recoloured Candle X).
- Imply endorsement by, or affiliation with, ExTerm Labs.
- Remove or alter the copyright and license notices in the source, the `LICENSE` file, or the in-app About notice.

## Forks must

1. Choose a different name and logo, and replace the Candle X mark and the `@exterm.labs` watermark in the UI and in
   generated images (flex cards, Share Setup and Daily Recap PNGs).
2. Credit the original clearly, for example: *"Based on ExTerm — https://github.com/uexxx/ExTerm"*.
3. Stay under the GNU AGPL-3.0 and publish their complete corresponding source, including when offered over a network.

## Official builds

Official releases are published only at <https://github.com/uexxx/ExTerm/releases> and ship a `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
See [How to verify official builds](README.md#-how-to-verify-official-builds).

Questions about brand use: open an issue at <https://github.com/uexxx/ExTerm/issues>.
